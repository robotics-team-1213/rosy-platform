## Decisions and alternatives

- Keep BatteryMonitor/SAF-005 display and shutdown behavior unchanged. A separate private power source retains original energy; embedding assumed current/capacity in the display monitor would invent evidence and alter an unrelated safety path.
- Do not multiply voltage/SOC/nameplate capacity into an asserted usable-energy bound. The supported source must supply a calibrated **remaining usable energy lower bound**, already accounting for its measurement uncertainty, discharge cutoff, temperature and load envelope. Its SOH/capacity revision is measured provenance, not the displayed percentage.
- Source provider identity is an exact trusted in-process port object bound by private composition. A public BatteryEnergyEvidence DTO or string `source=bms` cannot supply this identity. The source kind is constrained to bms/fuel_gauge/bench; unknown/voltage_only are closed. The bench test profile is explicitly SYNTHETIC.
- Whole-device upper power includes idle/ZERO/waiting, robot computation/sensors, return, dock/retries and stop tail. Unsupported/unknown bounds remain closed. Fleet still must establish the actual work/return route and whole-window duration/cost/context; this restriction alone proves neither return feasibility nor completion.

## Private values and source

`middleware/core/services/core_features/power/execution_energy.py` owns frozen, ROS-free profile/reading/receipt values and `PackEnergySource`. Exact names may change for local consistency, but these invariants may not:

1. Profile binds robot/CORE boot UUID, battery ID, source/provider identity, source epoch, capacity revision, policy/profile revision, whole-device power ceiling W, strictly positive reserve J, maximum original sample age, minimum SOH, temperature interval. Reserve J belongs to this accepted immutable profile, with no consumer-selected default. Its provenance must be accepted alongside the whole-device W bound. All bounds/identifiers are finite/bounded and immutable. A new pack/source epoch/capacity revision is unsupported in the active boot profile; no old parent is rebound to it.
2. Reading is a strict immutable scalar record: original sequence and source stamp ns, battery/source epoch/capacity identity, calibrated remaining usable Wh lower bound, measured SOC/SOH/temperature and fault. It is not a public DTO or a type tag that authenticates hardware. An actual future supported port must produce these fields with accepted calibration; no current ordinary bridge creates a positive reading.
3. Source `begin(provider)` returns an exact one-use read ticket and captures the monotonic lower bracket before parsing. Complete takes the original reading, original source-now ns and final read bracket. Clock/header reversal, future stamp, wrong provider/scope, malformed/unknown values, fault, insufficient SOH or unsafe temperature invalidate the current source. Same sequence/header with changed facts is rejected; old tokens cannot replace newer source state. Sequence and header watermarks are retained, not a growing per-message history.
4. Project source age into monotonic capture conservatively: capture lower bound = before minus age (round down); original expiry = capture + profile max age (round down). Include the entire read/validation delay in the final freshness check. A duplicate can only retain/shrink its original capture/expiry, never renew it; after expiry/failure that original cannot revive. Only a strictly newer genuine sequence/header can become a fresh receipt. A fresh measured bound may increase after physical charging; that does not restart any existing Budget or selected receipt. It must not be synthesized by increasing a receipt timestamp.
5. A pending read closes **new parent admission** until completed. Existing selected motion may use an unexpired previously validated sample during bounded read processing; malformed/fault completion returns invalid source facts for CORE publication; CORE owns the captured-parent STOP latch. Source processing does not independently publish a motion-positive cache. CORE retains at most 8 still-unexpired published receipt identities; overflow closes rather than dropping a selected live identity. Cache inspection for selection/final submission takes no source lock, callbacks or clock.
6. `core/bridge/pack_energy.py` is the ROS-free adaptation/hand-over sibling: serialize ingestion through a dedicated adapter lock, capture exact source and parent before conversion, feed the private read ticket, then hand immutable results to CORE. On failure request STOP only for the captured parent. Stale callbacks never clear a replacement source or redirect STOP. Ordinary battery/voltage and batt_state callbacks stay unchanged; guessed BatteryState fields are not a positive energy adapter.

## Full-window checks and ownership

`core/drive/execution.py` installs the exact source/profile once before the first cycle. Native-domain Fence has an energy REQUIRED/CLOSED slot from native installation, just as the native-map slot. Partial/failed installation remains closed, with no native-without-energy option. The declared-metadata test Fence without native owners and the ordinary uninstalled factory remain distinct existing paths.

Parent admission must validate energy before and after adopting the same original Budget. Capture receipt before fresh ledger clock, verify source is not pending and still installed, same robot/boot/profile/pack epoch/capacity, current health/freshness, accepted power equals the Budget Limits.whole_load_w. Do not renew initial admission deadline or source capture during slow validation. Failure consumes/rejects this attempted parent and returns no positive handle. Preserve the ledger's existing parent/run capacity and ownership rules.

Use directed rounding and strict reserve inequality. A parent Budget's original start+duration is its full permitted end, already including the physical stopping tail enforced by Budget.remaining. Required energy from a measurement is the whole-device power times max(0, original parent end minus original energy capture lower bound), plus immutable reserve J. Available is the conservatively converted remaining usable Wh lower bound ×3600. Equality/overflow/nonfinite values deny. Idle time before a later parent therefore consumes the **same measurement**; child replacement, parent retirement, lease renewal and another admission cannot reset its capture or debit. A genuinely new original measurement is a new physical lower bound, not renewal of an old one.

### Publication and final-writer ordering

Source owns original immutable physical facts; CORE Fence owns published energy context and parent-specific insufficiency/fault STOP. The private adapter is the only supported ingress into the installed source. It holds its ingestion lock from begin through CORE publication, so a later positive reading cannot overtake an earlier low/fault result. Under modes->ledger it first marks a pending read and captures exact source/parent, then releases that boundary for conversion/source validation. Source locks are released before acquiring modes->ledger for publication. No path under modes->ledger waits for adapter/source locks. New parent admission observes pending and denies; an existing parent may use its previous finite proof while processing remains pending.

Completion publication acquires modes->ledger, rechecks exact installed source and captured parent, evaluates the immutable result against that parent's original fixed end and accepted profile, and sets its monotonic STOP request BEFORE publishing the replacement cache and clearing pending. Invalid/low publication consumes that parent even if a genuinely newer healthy sample arrives before the next writer. An old callback cannot publish into a replacement source or target a replacement parent. Pending is scoped by exact ticket/source identity; stale completion cannot clear another read's pending state. No source result alone grants output authority. Internal raw processing state is not read by the final predicate; only coherently published CORE state is used. Expiry is checked independently on every writer using fresh ledger time.

Publication and final submit share the existing modes->ledger boundary. A writer that already holds the boundary and passes its final checks is ordered before any completion waiting to publish. Its single raw write may finish; completion is published immediately after obtaining the boundary and prevents subsequent writes. Do not claim cancellation of a write already entered, bounded lock latency, or physical stop. The submission linearization point is the successful final checks under that boundary immediately before entering the existing raw writer. Deterministic tests hold final-check->fault-processing->submit, assert at most that already-entered write, then publication->next-write ZERO; and low-publication->higher-publication->next-writer must stay STOPPED. A malformed result that cannot complete still expires via original TTL; no timeout renewal.

At native entry/readiness/receipt synchronization, selection and final submit require this energy proof. A selected Context retains its original receipt/deadline. A newer positive reading cannot extend it or revive a consumed parent. Also check the latest CORE-published receipt/health and its remaining bound so a new lower-energy/fault observation cannot be hidden behind a still-fresh old selection. A normal fresh source update with sufficient energy must not itself consume the parent. Source-pointer replacement, profile/epoch/capacity mismatch, clock fault, expiry, low energy or loss consumes the captured parent.

Final path uses only immutable/cached values and the existing fresh ledger clock under modes→ledger nonblocking boundary. No source/owner locks, getter callbacks, ROS/RMW, hashing, file/network I/O, events or mode transitions enter it. Existing supported CPython3.12/GIL restriction and independent OMX owner remain unchanged. Software STOP is not measured physical stop.
