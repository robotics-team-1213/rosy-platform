## 정확한 프롬프트

Use case: logo-brand. Create a refined modern identity overview for ROSY, an actual robotics platform coordinating robot devices, Fleet control and AI situation interpretation. One original, ownable geometric R monogram: a continuous thick precision route with a rounded upper loop and a decisive diagonal lower leg, simple open negative space, readable at 16 pixels. The mark conveys observe, decide, execute as one coherent route. Serious engineering, clear human control, confident and calm. Avoid robot faces, flowers, gear icons, hexagon cages, circuit spaghetti, generic AI sparkles, gradients, glow, 3D effects, and copied company logos. Use a single consistent mark throughout a beautifully composed compact 2x2 brand board, wide landscape: top-left large rose mark plus exact word 'ROSY' in exceptionally refined custom geometric sans; top-right same mark as white and black monochrome; bottom-left same logo on four simple app icon tiles with subtle role dots representing Fleet, Robot, Pilot, Cam; bottom-right a restrained desktop control header showing the small same mark and exact text 'ROSY Fleet', elegant icon-only controls. Brand colors already established and must be respected: rose #f697e7 against charcoal #101214, deep rose #a33391 on off-white #eeeeef; white/black monochrome examples. Grid alignment, generous disciplined whitespace, crisp flat vector-like edges, professional Swiss engineering identity, aesthetically striking without ornament. Minimal board copy: only ROSY and ROSY Fleet, no invented slogans or nonsense labels. All panels use exactly the SAME R geometry. This is a concept reference intended for a production SVG logo and real UI assets, not a poster illustration.
